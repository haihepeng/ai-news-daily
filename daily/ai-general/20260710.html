Anthropic 发现 Claude 内部隐藏的"思考空间"J-lens

#Anthropic #Claude #可解释性 #AI安全 #研究 · 来源：

Anthropic developed 'Jacobian lens' (J-lens), a mechanistic interpretability technique revealing a hidden space inside Claude Opus 4.6 where the model 'ponders' related concepts before outputting responses. The J-space contains words related to what the model is likely to say next — effectively revealing what's 'on its mind.' Anthropic found that what an LLM is actually doing can often be different from what it says it is doing. The company partnered with Neuronpedia for public demos. The technique builds on previous mechanistic interpretability work and exposes a deeper level inside LLMs researchers had not seen before.

推荐理由：J-lens技术对LLM可解释性和安全性研究有重大意义。建议关注J-lens的开源进展和 Neuronpedia的公测体验，以及对AI安全研究的推动作用。

Google 将在广告中心披露 AI 生成广告

#Google #AI广告 #透明度 #监管 #消费者保护 · 来源：

Google is rolling out a new feature in 'My Ad Center' panel letting users know when an ad was made using AI technology. Users see an option 'how this ad was made' indicating if the ad was created or edited with AI. When advertisers use Google's own generative AI advertising tools, the disclosure is automatically enabled. However, if the ad is created elsewhere, advertisers will need to self-disclose — Google will not perform its own check. This feature is available globally on Google Search, YouTube, and Google Discover. Previously only election ads were required to disclose AI use.

推荐理由：Google的AI广告披露政策是AI透明度的重要进展。建议关注其他平台的跟进情况以及监管机构对AI生成内容的具体要求。
